Is QBLOCK free?

QBLOCK is being made to be easy to jump into. More details about access and availability will be shared as the world gets closer.

Can I create my own games?

Creating is at the heart of QBLOCK. We’re shaping the tools so your ideas can become experiences other players want to play.

Will there be a PC version?

We’re exploring the best ways to bring QBLOCK to more players. Keep an eye on the community for the latest updates.

Can I play with friends?

Absolutely. QBLOCK is made for shared adventures, friendly competition, and those “one more round” moments.

How often is QBLOCK updated?

The world will keep growing with new games, ideas, and player feedback. Join Discord to follow along.
